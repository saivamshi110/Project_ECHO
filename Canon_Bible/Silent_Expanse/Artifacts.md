# Artifacts of the Silent Expanse

## Overview

Artifacts are objects created by the Elyrians before the Great Silence. They range from simple personal belongings to sophisticated Echo-powered devices that continue to function thousands of years later.

Every artifact represents a piece of Elyrian history and provides insight into their knowledge, technology, and culture.

While many artifacts possess practical value, others exist solely to preserve the legacy of a civilization that vanished.

---

# Classification

Modern researchers classify Elyrian artifacts into five primary categories.

---

# Knowledge Artifacts

Objects designed to preserve information and history.

Examples include:

- Memory Crystals
- Star Charts
- Historical Records
- Research Journals
- Echo Archives
- Language Tablets

These artifacts are invaluable to scholars seeking to understand the Elyrians.

---

# Utility Artifacts

Devices created to assist with everyday tasks and exploration.

Examples include:

- Echo Keys
- Navigation Compasses
- Energy Cells
- Access Tokens
- Portable Light Sources
- Mechanical Tools

Many Elyrian facilities cannot be accessed without these devices.

---

# Scientific Artifacts

Advanced instruments used for research and experimentation.

Examples include:

- Echo Resonators
- Energy Analyzers
- Gravity Stabilizers
- Atmospheric Sensors
- Experimental Prototypes

Modern civilizations understand only a fraction of their true capabilities.

---

# Power Artifacts

Rare devices capable of manipulating Echo Energy.

Known functions include:

- Amplifying Echo Energy
- Stabilizing unstable regions
- Activating ancient machinery
- Enhancing physical abilities
- Protecting against Echo anomalies

These artifacts are exceptionally rare and often heavily guarded.

---

# Cultural Artifacts

Objects reflecting the daily lives of the Elyrians.

Examples include:

- Sculptures
- Jewelry
- Musical Instruments
- Ceremonial Masks
- Paintings
- Household Items

Although they possess little military value, they provide historians with invaluable cultural insight.

---

# Preservation

Artifacts have survived for thousands of years due to the extraordinary craftsmanship of the Elyrians and the stabilizing properties of Echo Energy.

Many remain in remarkable condition despite the passage of time.

---

# Recovery

Modern expeditions recover artifacts for various reasons.

Primary objectives include:

- Historical research
- Scientific study
- Museum preservation
- Technological advancement
- Commercial value

Not every recovered artifact is understood.

---

# Dangers

Some artifacts remain unstable.

Potential risks include:

- Echo Energy surges
- Temporary spatial distortions
- Activation of ancient defense systems
- Unexpected machinery responses
- Harmful energy emissions

Improper handling has resulted in numerous expedition failures.

---

# Gameplay Role

Artifacts play a central role in exploration and progression throughout **ECHO: The Living World**.

Players may use artifacts to:

- Unlock hidden areas
- Solve environmental puzzles
- Access Elyrian facilities
- Discover forgotten history
- Upgrade equipment
- Progress the main story
- Complete optional quests

Some legendary artifacts are unique and may significantly influence the narrative.
