# Timeline of the Silent Expanse

## Overview

The following timeline summarizes the known historical events related to the Silent Expanse.

Exact dates remain uncertain due to the loss of Elyrian historical records.

---

# Age of the Elyrians

- Rise of the Elyrian civilization.
- Discovery and mastery of Echo Energy.
- Construction of cities, observatories, temples, and research facilities.
- Expansion across the region.

---

# The Great Silence

- Elyrian civilization disappears.
- Echo anomalies begin appearing.
- Modern historical records become fragmented.

---

# Age of Isolation

- The region becomes abandoned.
- Early kingdoms avoid the Expanse.
- Rumors and legends spread.

---

# Age of Exploration

- Organized expeditions begin.
- First artifacts recovered.
- Ancient ruins rediscovered.

---

# Modern Era

- Active scientific research.
- Kingdom-sponsored expeditions.
- Continued exploration despite significant risks.

---
