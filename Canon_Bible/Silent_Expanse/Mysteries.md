# Mysteries of the Silent Expanse

## Overview

Although centuries of research have revealed fragments of Elyrian history, the Silent Expanse remains filled with unanswered questions.

Some mysteries may eventually be solved.

Others continue to challenge every generation of explorers.

---

# Major Mysteries

## The Great Silence

No confirmed explanation exists for the disappearance of the Elyrians.

---

## The Origin of Echo Energy

No scholar understands where Echo Energy originated.

---

## The Heart of Silence

No verified expedition has reached its center and returned with complete records.

---

## The Living Machines

Many Elyrian constructs continue operating despite thousands of years without maintenance.

---

## Floating Structures

Entire buildings remain suspended above the landscape through unknown mechanisms.

---

## Echo Storms

Their exact cause remains unknown.

---

## Lost Cities

Historical records describe cities that have never been found.

---

## Disappearing Expeditions

Some expeditions vanish without leaving any trace.

---

# Scholarly Debate

Competing theories exist for nearly every major mystery.

No single explanation has been universally accepted.

---

# Gameplay Role

Mysteries encourage:

- Exploration
- Investigation
- Environmental storytelling
- Side quests
- Main story progression

Not every mystery is intended to be solved immediately.

---

# Secret Canon Notice

The definitive answers to these mysteries are documented separately within the **Secret_Canon** folder and are not considered public knowledge within the world.
