# Creatures of the Silent Expanse

## Overview

The Silent Expanse is home to a wide variety of creatures that have adapted to its unstable environment. While some species are native to the region, others have been altered through long-term exposure to Echo Energy.

Not every creature is hostile. Some avoid human contact, while others become aggressive only when threatened or influenced by Echo phenomena.

Understanding local wildlife is considered essential for any successful expedition.

---

# Creature Classification

Modern researchers classify creatures into five primary groups.

## Native Wildlife

Animals that naturally inhabited the region before the Great Silence and have adapted over time.

Examples include:

- Mountain Stags
- Stone Rams
- Cave Foxes
- Ash Wolves
- Crystal Falcons

These creatures generally avoid conflict unless provoked.

---

## Echo-Touched Creatures

Animals permanently changed by prolonged exposure to Echo Energy.

Common characteristics include:

- Glowing markings
- Enhanced strength
- Increased intelligence
- Altered senses
- Unusual abilities

Many retain their original instincts despite these changes.

---

## Corrupted Creatures

Some organisms have become dangerously unstable after extreme Echo exposure.

Typical traits include:

- Aggressive behavior
- Physical mutations
- Unpredictable movement
- High resistance to damage
- Unstable Echo emissions

These creatures are considered highly dangerous.

---

## Ancient Constructs

Mechanical guardians created by the Elyrians.

Although thousands of years old, many remain operational.

Examples include:

- Sentinel Units
- Gate Guardians
- Archive Protectors
- Survey Drones
- Fortress Defenders

Most continue carrying out their original purpose.

---

## Apex Creatures

Rare and powerful beings that dominate entire regions.

Characteristics include:

- Exceptional intelligence
- Unique abilities
- Territorial behavior
- Significant influence on surrounding ecosystems

Many expeditions avoid these creatures entirely.

---

# Ecosystem

Despite its dangers, the Silent Expanse maintains a functioning ecosystem.

Predators, prey, scavengers, and plant life exist in a delicate balance shaped by Echo Energy.

Major Echo Storms can temporarily disrupt this balance.

---

# Relationship with Echo Energy

Different species react differently to Echo Energy.

Some adapt.

Some evolve.

Some become corrupted.

Others appear completely unaffected.

Researchers continue studying these variations.

---

# Human Interaction

Expeditions encounter creatures in many ways.

Possible interactions include:

- Observation
- Combat
- Avoidance
- Tracking
- Scientific research
- Conservation efforts

Not every encounter requires violence.

---

# Gameplay Role

Creatures contribute to exploration and world-building by providing:

- Combat encounters
- Environmental storytelling
- Hunting opportunities
- Resource collection
- Quest objectives
- Rare discoveries

Creature behavior changes according to region, weather, and Echo activity.
