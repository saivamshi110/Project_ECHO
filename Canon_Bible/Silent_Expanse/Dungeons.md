# Dungeons of the Silent Expanse

## Overview

The Silent Expanse contains countless underground complexes, forgotten temples, research facilities, and hidden strongholds built by the Elyrians.

These locations are collectively referred to as **Dungeons** by modern explorers.

Each dungeon preserves a fragment of Elyrian history while presenting unique challenges to those who enter.

No two dungeons are identical.

---

# Purpose

Every dungeon was originally built to serve a specific function within Elyrian society.

Some were places of knowledge.

Others produced energy.

Some protected important secrets.

After the Great Silence, these structures became isolated, dangerous, and largely forgotten.

# Dungeon Structure

Although every dungeon is unique, most contain several key areas.

- Entrance
- Exploration Zone
- Puzzle Chambers
- Combat Areas
- Hidden Rooms
- Main Objective
- Reward Chamber
- Exit Route

Some dungeons contain multiple interconnected levels.

---

# Common Hazards

Explorers may encounter:

- Echo Storm interference
- Ancient traps
- Collapsing structures
- Flooded passages
- Corrupted creatures
- Active Elyrian constructs
- Environmental puzzles

Preparation is often more important than combat ability.

---

# Rewards

Successful expeditions may recover:

- Elyrian artifacts
- Rare crafting materials
- Historical documents
- Echo-powered technology
- Ancient equipment
- Secret passages
- Story discoveries

Some rewards permanently unlock new exploration opportunities elsewhere in the world.

---

# Exploration Philosophy

Dungeons are designed to reward curiosity.

Players who investigate hidden paths, solve optional puzzles, and observe environmental details will often discover additional lore, equipment, or secret areas.

Not every reward is material; some provide deeper understanding of the world and its history.

---

# Gameplay Role

Dungeons serve as the primary locations for:

- Main story missions
- Side quests
- Boss encounters
- Puzzle challenges
- Artifact recovery
- Character progression
- Lore exploration

Each dungeon should offer a distinct atmosphere and memorable experience.
