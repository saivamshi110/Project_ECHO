# Silent Expanse – Developer Notes

> Internal design reference for Project ECHO.

---

# Design Goals

The Silent Expanse should inspire:

- Wonder
- Curiosity
- Mystery
- Discovery
- Isolation
- Respect for the unknown

Players should feel like explorers rather than conquerors.

---

# World Design Principles

- Exploration should always be rewarded.
- Environmental storytelling should be prioritized over exposition.
- Every major ruin should have a believable historical purpose.
- Mysteries should encourage curiosity rather than confusion.
- Echo Energy should remain fascinating rather than becoming ordinary.

---

# Gameplay Principles

The Silent Expanse should balance:

- Combat
- Exploration
- Puzzle solving
- Platforming
- Narrative discovery
- Resource management

No single activity should dominate the player's experience.

---

# Narrative Principles

The player gradually uncovers history through:

- Ruins
- Artifacts
- Journals
- NPC dialogue
- Environmental clues

Avoid excessive exposition.

Allow players to connect the pieces themselves.

---

# Future Expansion

Potential additions include:

- New regions
- Additional dungeons
- Legendary artifacts
- Optional bosses
- Seasonal Echo phenomena
- DLC content
