# Gameplay

## Overview

The Crystal Vault focuses on preservation, synchronization, exploration, and environmental restoration. Players manipulate living Resonance Crystals to stabilize Echo Energy throughout the Vault.

Unlike traditional treasure vaults, the Crystal Vault rewards understanding rather than collection.

---

## Core Gameplay Loop

Explore Crystal Chambers

↓

Discover Resonance Nodes

↓

Synchronize Crystal Networks

↓

Solve Energy Routing Puzzles

↓

Restore Crystal Ecosystems

↓

Unlock Hidden Vaults

↓

Strengthen Living Resonance Network

---

## Primary Gameplay Systems

### Crystal Synchronization

Players activate dormant Resonance Crystals to restore energy flow.

---

### Crystal Growth

Living crystals expand naturally after synchronization.

New pathways, bridges, and hidden chambers become accessible.

---

### Energy Routing

Players redirect Echo Energy through crystalline conduits.

Incorrect routing causes Resonance instability.

---

### Crystal Stabilization

Some ancient crystals have become unstable.

Players restore balance using Resonance abilities learned in previous locations.

---

### Exploration Rewards

Exploration reveals:

- Hidden lore
- Crystal Seeds
- Ancient research
- Rare Resonance materials
- Secret chambers

---

## Gameplay Philosophy

The Crystal Vault reinforces one principle:

**Protect the source before using its power.**
