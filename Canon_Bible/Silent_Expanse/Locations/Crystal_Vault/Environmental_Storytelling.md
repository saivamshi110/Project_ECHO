# Environmental Storytelling

The Crystal Vault tells the story of the Elyrians' relationship with Resonance Crystals.

Rather than explaining their technology directly, the environment reveals centuries of crystal cultivation and preservation.

---

## Crystal Gardens

Large crystal formations demonstrate how Resonance Crystals naturally grow when supplied with stable Echo Energy.

---

## Research Chambers

Scientific workspaces contain preserved experiments documenting the discovery of crystal synchronization.

---

## Ancient Workshops

Tools and cultivation devices reveal that Resonance Crystals were carefully grown rather than mined.

---

## Echo Imprints

Players occasionally witness brief memories of Elyrian scientists cultivating new crystal species.

---

## Restoration

As players restore the Vault:

- Crystal forests regrow.
- Echo Light returns.
- Resonance storms disappear.
- Ancient machinery awakens.
