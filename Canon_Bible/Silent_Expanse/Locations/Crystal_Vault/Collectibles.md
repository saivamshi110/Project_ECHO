# Collectibles

## Crystal Seeds

The earliest stage of a Living Resonance Crystal.

Purpose:

- Upgrade Crystal Resonance.
- Improve synchronization.

---

## Resonance Shards

Fragments released from damaged crystals.

Purpose:

- Equipment upgrades.
- Puzzle activation.

---

## Scientific Journals

Research notes describing crystal cultivation.

Unlock:

- Lore entries.
- World history.

---

## Ancient Crystal Samples

Rare preserved crystal species.

Purpose:

- Museum collection.
- Achievement progress.
