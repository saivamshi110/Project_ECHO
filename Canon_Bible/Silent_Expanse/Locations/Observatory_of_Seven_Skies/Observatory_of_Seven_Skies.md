# Observatory of Seven Skies

## Overview

The Observatory of Seven Skies stands atop the highest peaks of the Skyfracture Highlands, where floating islands drift above an endless sea of clouds. It was the Elyrians' greatest center for astronomical observation, celestial navigation, and long-range Echo monitoring.

Far more than a simple observatory, it was designed to understand the relationship between the stars, Echo Energy, and the natural balance of Aethera.

Even after thousands of years, its massive telescopes continue to move silently across the heavens.

Many explorers believe the Observatory watches something beyond the night sky.

# Historical Purpose

The Observatory served multiple scientific purposes.

Its scholars studied:

- Celestial bodies
- Planetary movements
- Echo Energy fluctuations
- Weather systems
- Long-distance navigation
- Cosmic phenomena
- Seasonal cycles

It also functioned as the primary navigation center for Elyrian expeditions across Aethera.

---

# History

Constructed during the height of Elyrian civilization, the Observatory became one of the most respected scientific institutions in the world.

Researchers gathered observations continuously for centuries, creating the most accurate star maps ever known.

During the Great Silence, every observation stopped instantly.

The telescopes, however, never stopped moving.

To this day, they continue tracking invisible patterns across the sky.

No one understands why.

# Layout

The Observatory contains seven major sections.

## 1. Mountain Ascent

Players climb ancient paths leading toward the floating observatory.

Introduces traversal mechanics.

---

## 2. Navigation Hall

Displays ancient celestial maps.

Players solve navigation-based puzzles.

---

## 3. Telescope Gallery

Massive telescopes dominate the chamber.

Players manipulate their positions to unlock hidden pathways.

---

## 4. Celestial Library

Contains astronomical records and Echo star charts.

Focuses on exploration and lore.

---

## 5. Sky Bridges

A network of suspended bridges connecting floating towers.

Platforming becomes increasingly challenging.

---

## 6. Resonance Dome

A massive chamber that amplifies celestial Echo signals.

Environmental puzzles revolve around light and sound.

---

## 7. Crown Observatory

The highest point of the structure.

Here, players confront the Observatory's final guardian while overlooking the entirety of the Silent Expanse.
