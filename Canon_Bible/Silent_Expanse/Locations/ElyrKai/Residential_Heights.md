# Residential Heights

## Overview

Residential Heights was home to the majority of Elyr'Kai's citizens. Built across elevated terraces overlooking the capital, the district combined elegant architecture with gardens, schools, markets, and community spaces.

Unlike the Government Quarter or Scholar District, this area existed for ordinary life.

Children played here.

Families gathered here.

Neighbors celebrated together here.

The district reminds players that the Elyrians were not simply inventors and rulers—they were people.

# Design Philosophy

The district should create a feeling rarely found in ruined cities:

**A life interrupted.**

Instead of focusing on destruction, the environment should suggest that everyday routines suddenly stopped.

Players should feel both warmth and sadness while exploring.

---

# Historical Purpose

Residential Heights served as the primary living district for Elyr'Kai.

It included:

- Family homes
- Apartments
- Community gardens
- Schools
- Clinics
- Local markets
- Neighborhood libraries
- Recreation areas

Every profession in the city was represented here.

# Layout

## 1. Sunrise Gate

The eastern entrance overlooking the valley below.

The first rays of daylight illuminate the district every morning.

---

## 2. Market Square

A neighborhood marketplace where local artisans and farmers traded goods.

Echo memories occasionally replay ordinary conversations and daily routines.

---

## 3. Learning Courtyard

Children once attended community schools here.

Players discover educational devices, games, and student artwork preserved by Echo Energy.

---

## 4. Garden Walk

A series of elevated parks connected by bridges and water channels.

Minimal combat encourages exploration.

---

## 5. Family Quarter

Rows of homes showing how Elyrian families lived.

Environmental storytelling is the focus.

---

## 6. Community Hall

A public gathering place used for celebrations, festivals, and neighborhood meetings.

Hidden Echo memories reveal moments from everyday life.

---

## 7. Skyline Terrace

The highest residential overlook in the city.

Players can see nearly every major district of Elyr'Kai from this location.

# Gameplay

Primary gameplay emphasizes:

- Exploration
- Environmental storytelling

# Environmental Storytelling

Players may discover:

- Family journals
- Children's toys
- Musical instruments
- Dinner tables left prepared
- Letters between neighbors
- Graduation photographs
- Garden journals
- Community notice boards

These details reveal the human side of Elyrian civilization.

---

# Enemies

Common enemies include:

- Echo Phantoms
- Crystal Growths
- Lost Civic Constructs
- Garden Sentinels

Enemy encounters are limited and localized to preserve the district's atmosphere.

---

# Major Landmark

## Community Hall

The emotional center of Residential Heights.

Echo memories replay weddings, festivals, concerts, and neighborhood celebrations.

Players experience the joy of Elyrian society before confronting the tragedy of its disappearance.

# Story Role

Residential Heights changes the player's relationship with the Elyrians.

Until now, they have explored institutions.

Here, they discover homes.

Instead of asking:

*"What did the Elyrians build?"*

Players begin asking:

*"Who were these people?"*
