# Echo Gardens

## Overview

The Echo Gardens were the ecological heart of Elyr'Kai, where nature and Echo Energy existed in deliberate harmony. Rather than serving as ornamental parks, the Gardens functioned as living research sanctuaries dedicated to preserving biodiversity, restoring ecosystems, and studying the interaction between life and Echo Energy.

Here, the Elyrians cultivated plants that could only exist in environments enriched by Echo resonance. Rare species from every region of Aethera were protected within carefully balanced habitats.

Although centuries have passed since the Great Silence, much of the Gardens continues to thrive under automated ecological systems.

The result is both beautiful and unsettling.

Nature has survived.

Humanity has not.

# Design Philosophy

The Echo Gardens should inspire:

- Peace
- Wonder
- Quiet sadness

This district provides an emotional pause between more dangerous areas while still rewarding exploration.

# Historical Purpose

The Gardens existed to:

- Preserve endangered species
- Research Echo-assisted agriculture
- Study ecosystems
- Develop medicinal plants
- Restore damaged environments
- Educate citizens about ecology

Researchers from the Scholar District frequently collaborated with botanists here.

# Layout

## 1. Garden Gate

The entrance lined with ancient flowering trees that still bloom every season.

---

## 2. Botanical Conservatory

A massive glass structure housing rare plant species from across Aethera.

Players solve light-based environmental puzzles by redirecting Echo energy through living plants.

---

## 3. Crystal River

A flowing waterway enriched by Echo Crystals.

Its unusual properties support unique flora and fauna found nowhere else.

---

## 4. Arboretum of Giants

A forest of colossal ancient trees whose roots intertwine with Echo crystal formations.

Vertical exploration becomes the primary gameplay mechanic.

---

## 5. Medicinal Grove

Where healers cultivated plants used throughout Elyrian society.

Players discover recipes, botanical records, and optional upgrades.

---

## 6. Pollinator Sanctuary

An open habitat filled with Echo-adapted insects, birds, and floating organisms.

Most wildlife is peaceful unless disturbed.

---

## 7. Heart Grove

The symbolic center of the Gardens.

An immense luminous tree, sustained by centuries of Echo Energy, dominates the landscape.

Its roots extend beneath much of Elyr'Kai.

# Major Landmark

## Heart Grove

The largest living organism known in Aethera.

Its immense Echo-infused roots stabilize portions of the city's ecological systems.

The tree is not magical in the traditional sense—it is the result of centuries of botanical science and careful cultivation.

# Story Role

The Echo Gardens reveal another side of the Elyrians.

Their civilization was not built through endless expansion or exploitation.

Instead, they invested enormous effort into understanding and protecting the natural world.

This district challenges the assumption that technological advancement must come at nature's expense.
