# Council Citadel

## Overview

The Council Citadel stands at the highest point of Elyr'Kai, overlooking every district of the capital. It served as the headquarters of the Council of Seven—the leaders entrusted with guiding Elyrian civilization through wisdom, scientific progress, ethical debate, and long-term stewardship.

Unlike the palaces of ancient kingdoms, the Citadel was never designed to glorify a single ruler. Every chamber, corridor, and council hall reflects the Elyrian belief that no individual should stand above knowledge, responsibility, or the common good.

Today, the Citadel is silent. Automated systems continue to function, Echo memories linger in its halls, and the final decisions of the Council remain locked behind ancient security systems.

For the player, this is where the truth behind the Great Silence begins to unfold.

# Layout

## 1. Citadel Gate

A massive entrance protected by advanced Echo security systems.

Players unlock access only after restoring key systems throughout Elyr'Kai.

---

## 2. Hall of Unity

A ceremonial hall displaying the history of the Council of Seven.

Interactive Echo memories reveal pivotal moments in Elyrian history.

---

## 3. Chamber of Ethics

A meeting room where controversial scientific proposals were debated.

Environmental storytelling highlights the civilization's commitment to responsibility over ambition.

---

## 4. Strategic Operations Center

The central command room for responding to disasters, coordinating expeditions, and monitoring infrastructure across Aethera.

Players reactivate portions of its holographic world map.

---

## 5. Council Archives

A secure repository containing classified records, diplomatic agreements, and historical decisions.

Many documents are encrypted and require special access.

---

## 6. Chamber of Seven

The primary council chamber.

Seven identical seats surround a circular table, symbolizing equality among the council members.

No seat is elevated above another.

---

## 7. Apex Observatory

The highest accessible point in Elyr'Kai.

From here, players can see the entire capital and many distant landmarks across Aethera.

The final Echo memory before entering the Origin Chamber is witnessed here.

# Story Role

The Council Citadel reveals that the Elyrians did not fall because of greed, corruption, or civil war.

Instead, they faced an impossible crisis that tested every principle on which their civilization had been built.

The player leaves the Citadel understanding not only what happened, but why the Council chose the path it did.
