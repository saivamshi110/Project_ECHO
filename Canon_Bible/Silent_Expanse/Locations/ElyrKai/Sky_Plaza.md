# Sky Plaza

## Overview

Sky Plaza was the cultural and ceremonial heart of Elyr'Kai. Suspended high above the city's lower districts, it served as the gathering place where citizens celebrated achievements, honored history, welcomed visitors, and looked toward the stars.

Unlike the Government Quarter, where laws shaped society, or the Scholar District, where ideas were born, Sky Plaza existed to strengthen the bonds between people.

It represented the spirit of Elyrian civilization.

# Design Philosophy

Sky Plaza should evoke:

- Awe
- Unity
- Celebration
- Hope

The environment should feel open, bright, and uplifting while carrying the quiet sadness of abandonment.

---

# Historical Purpose

The district hosted:

- Seasonal festivals
- Scientific exhibitions
- Graduation ceremonies
- Public concerts
- Diplomatic receptions
- Memorial gatherings
- Civic celebrations

Nearly every citizen of Elyr'Kai visited Sky Plaza at some point in their life.

# Layout

## 1. Ascension Stair

A monumental staircase leading upward from the lower districts.

Statues of Elyria's greatest scholars, engineers, explorers, and artists line the path.

---

## 2. Festival Square

The city's largest public square.

Echo memories replay moments of celebration, music, and community.

---

## 3. Hall of Harmony

An immense concert hall where musicians performed using Echo-powered instruments.

Players restore portions of the hall to unlock interactive musical puzzles.

---

## 4. Reflection Pools

Still pools that mirror the sky.

Environmental puzzles use reflections, light, and Echo resonance.

---

## 5. Heroes' Walk

A ceremonial avenue honoring individuals whose discoveries transformed Elyrian civilization.

Each monument tells an optional story.

---

## 6. Celestial Balcony

A panoramic overlook connecting visually with the Observatory of Seven Skies.

Players gain one of the best views in the entire game world.

---

## 7. Sky Crown

The symbolic centerpiece of Sky Plaza.

A floating circular platform powered by stabilized Echo Energy.

Before the Great Silence, it hosted the most important public ceremonies in Elyria.

# Gameplay

Primary gameplay includes:

- Exploration
- Environmental storytelling
- Reflection puzzles
- Musical puzzles
- Vertical traversal
- Hidden collectibles
- Light combat

Combat is intentionally limited.

The district emphasizes emotion and discovery.

---

# Environmental Storytelling

Players discover:

- Festival decorations
- Concert programs
- Diplomas
- Public speeches
- Children's artwork
- Memorial plaques
- Visitor journals
- Celebration banners

These details reveal what the Elyrians valued beyond science and technology.

---

# Enemies

Common encounters include:

- Echo Wisps
- Sky Sentinels
- Crystal Guardians
- Corrupted Ceremony Constructs

Enemy density remains low to preserve the district's atmosphere.

---

# Major Landmark

## Sky Crown

A magnificent floating platform overlooking all of Elyr'Kai.

As players restore Echo Energy throughout the city, sections of the platform gradually illuminate, symbolizing hope returning to a forgotten civilization.

Late in the story, Sky Crown becomes the location of a pivotal narrative event.

# Story Role

Sky Plaza demonstrates that Elyrian civilization celebrated knowledge, creativity, and community as much as power.

It provides emotional contrast to the loneliness found elsewhere in the Silent Expanse.
