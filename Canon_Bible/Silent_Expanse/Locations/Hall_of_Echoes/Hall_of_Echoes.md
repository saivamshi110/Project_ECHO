# Hall of Echoes

## Overview

The Hall of Echoes is one of the oldest and most sacred structures ever built by the Elyrians. It was not a government building, a research facility, or a place of worship. Instead, it served as the civilization's living memory.

Within its walls, Echo Resonance technology preserved experiences, emotions, discoveries, conversations, and historical events as interactive Echo Memories. Every citizen could contribute to this collective archive, ensuring that future generations could learn directly from the lives of those who came before them.

Unlike books, which record facts, the Hall of Echoes preserved perspective.

Today, countless memories remain suspended within its chambers, waiting to be awakened.

# Location Layout

## 1. Hall of Arrival

Visitors were welcomed here before beginning their journey through Elyrian history.

Interactive displays explained how Echo Memories were created.

---

## 2. Gallery of Lives

Thousands of floating Memory Orbs preserve moments from ordinary citizens.

Players witness birthdays, discoveries, friendships, festivals, and farewells.

The greatness of Elyria is shown through its people.

---

## 3. Chronicle Corridor

A chronological timeline of Elyrian history.

Major historical events can be experienced in sequence.

---

## 4. Chamber of Resonance

A massive chamber where memories were stabilized before being permanently archived.

Environmental puzzles revolve around restoring damaged resonance pathways.

---

## 5. Hall of Voices

Echo recordings preserve speeches, debates, poetry, music, and conversations.

Some memories respond differently depending on story progression.

---

## 6. Reflection Gallery

A quiet chamber where visitors contemplated memories they had experienced.

Mirrors of polished crystal project fragments of past events.

---

## 7. Resonance Core

The beating heart of the Hall.

A colossal Echo Crystal continuously synchronizes every surviving memory throughout the structure.

Its condition directly affects the stability of the Hall.

# Major Landmark

## Resonance Core

The largest Echo Crystal ever cultivated.

It continuously synchronized every preserved memory within the Hall, allowing experiences to remain stable for centuries.

Its restoration becomes a pivotal moment in the main story.

# Story Role

The Hall of Echoes fundamentally changes the player's understanding of the Elyrians.

Rather than learning through documents alone, players experience their triumphs, failures, hopes, and fears firsthand.

This location transforms the Elyrians from an ancient civilization into people the player can empathize with.
