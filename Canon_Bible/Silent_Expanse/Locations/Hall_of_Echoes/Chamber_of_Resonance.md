# Chamber of Resonance

## Overview

The Chamber of Resonance is the operational heart of the Hall of Echoes. Every memory preserved within the Hall passed through this chamber before becoming part of the permanent archive.

Here, Echo Resonance technology synchronized, stabilized, and verified memories to ensure they remained authentic and uncorrupted across generations.

Unlike ordinary archives, the Elyrians believed memories should never be altered. The Chamber therefore functioned as both a technological system and an ethical safeguard.

Today, centuries after the Great Silence, instability has spread throughout the Resonance Network. Memory pathways have fractured, synchronization arrays have failed, and corrupted Echo Energy threatens to erase portions of Elyrian history forever.

The player's mission is not to create new memories—but to restore the network that protects the old ones.

# Layout

## 1. Calibration Hall

The player learns how Echo Resonance devices operate.

Acts as an advanced tutorial for resonance mechanics.

---

## 2. Synchronization Array

Multiple rotating crystal rings maintain resonance frequencies.

Players align them to restore broken memory pathways.

---

## 3. Resonance Conduits

A network of suspended bridges carrying Echo Energy across the Hall.

Traversal challenges are introduced here.

---

## 4. Stability Nexus

The chamber's monitoring center.

Displays the health of every active Memory Archive.

Corrupted sectors become visible.

---

## 5. Echo Circuit Gallery

An educational area explaining the engineering principles behind Resonance technology.

Optional lore rewards curious players.

---

## 6. Central Resonance Engine

The largest machine inside the Hall.

A monumental crystal mechanism responsible for distributing synchronized Echo Energy throughout the entire archive.

Its restoration forms the climax of this location.
