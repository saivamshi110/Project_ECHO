# Hall of Echoes

> *"A civilization is remembered not only by what it built, but by what it chose to preserve."*

---

# Overview

The Hall of Echoes is one of the most significant Legendary Locations in **Project ECHO**. It serves as the world's greatest archive of memory, preserving the history, emotions, knowledge, and cultural legacy of the Elyrian civilization through the Living Resonance Network.

Rather than functioning as a traditional dungeon, the Hall emphasizes exploration, restoration, environmental storytelling, and narrative discovery. Players gradually uncover the civilization's past while repairing the systems that have safeguarded its memories for centuries.

The Hall also introduces several core gameplay systems that become foundational mechanics throughout Project ECHO, including the Reflection Memory System, Echo Preservation Engine, Living Resonance Network, and Resonance Synchronization.

---

# Narrative Role

The Hall of Echoes is the emotional and technological heart of the Silent Expanse.

It introduces the player to the Elyrians not as a vanished civilization, but as individuals whose lives, achievements, and sacrifices continue to shape the world.

The Hall concludes with the Final Trial, where the player earns the responsibility of becoming the next Keeper of Echoes.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Hall of Echoes is built around five principles:

1. Restoration over destruction.
2. Memory as a living experience.
3. Exploration rewards curiosity.
4. Architecture tells stories.
5. Progress is reflected in the world itself.

These principles guide every location, mechanic, and narrative moment within the Hall.
