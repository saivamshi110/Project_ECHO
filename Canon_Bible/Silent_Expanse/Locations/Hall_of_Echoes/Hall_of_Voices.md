# Hall of Voices

## Overview

The Hall of Voices preserves the spoken heritage of Elyrian civilization. While memories capture sights and emotions, this hall safeguards words—the speeches that united cities, the lectures that advanced science, the music that celebrated life, and the conversations that shaped history.

Every recording was voluntarily archived using Echo Resonance technology, allowing future generations to hear voices exactly as they were spoken.

The Elyrians believed that truth was not found only in events, but also in the words that inspired those events.

# Design Philosophy

The Hall of Voices should evoke:

- Intimacy
- Wisdom
- Inspiration
- Reflection
- Authenticity

Rather than overwhelming players with information, the Hall encourages them to pause, listen, and absorb the voices of a civilization that can no longer speak for itself.

---

# Historical Purpose

The Hall preserved:

- Council speeches
- Scientific lectures
- University lessons
- Musical performances
- Poetry recitals
- Diplomatic addresses
- Public debates
- Oral histories
- Personal messages

It ensured that future generations would hear not only what was said, but how it was said.

# Layout

## 1. Chamber of Welcome

Introduces visitors to the Hall and explains how spoken archives were preserved.

---

## 2. Council Auditorium

Contains preserved debates and public addresses from the Council of Seven.

Players begin to understand the personalities and philosophies of each council member.

---

## 3. Academy Forum

A collection of lectures from Elyria's greatest scholars, engineers, astronomers, and philosophers.

Some recordings unlock technical lore that helps solve puzzles elsewhere in the world.

---

## 4. Symphony Gallery

A vast chamber where music, choirs, and orchestral performances are preserved.

The room itself reacts dynamically to the rhythm of each performance.

---

## 5. Hall of Poetry

A tranquil gallery dedicated to literature, storytelling, and spoken art.

Many recordings reveal the emotional side of Elyrian culture.

---

## 6. Final Messages

Private recordings left during the final days before the Great Silence.

Most remain encrypted until late in the main story.

These recordings become some of the most emotional moments in the game.

# Gameplay

Primary gameplay includes:

- Narrative exploration
- Audio-based environmental puzzles
- Echo synchronization
- Hidden recordings
- Lore collection
- Optional investigations

Combat is almost entirely absent.

Silence is treated as an important design element.

Players discover:

- Lecture notes
- Musical instruments
- Performance programs
- Debate transcripts
- Recording crystals
- Audience seating logs
- Composer journals

These details reinforce the Hall's role as a cultural institution.

---

# Enemies

The Hall is exceptionally stable.

Only rare corruption appears:

- Echo Whispers
- Resonance Wisps
- Distorted Voices

These manifestations interfere with recordings rather than attacking directly.

---

# Major Landmark

## Grand Resonance Amphitheater

A magnificent circular auditorium capable of replaying speeches and performances exactly as they occurred centuries ago.

When restored, its acoustics fill the entire Hall with voices that have been silent for generations.

# Story Role

The Hall of Voices reveals the personalities behind Elyrian history.

Players hear leaders who questioned themselves, scientists who debated ethics, artists who celebrated beauty, and citizens who hoped their voices would endure beyond their own lifetimes.

This transforms historical figures into believable human beings.
