# Side Quests

## Overview

The Hall of Echoes contains optional quests that deepen the player's understanding of the Elyrian civilization. Rather than functioning as unrelated distractions, every side quest reinforces the Hall's central themes of memory, preservation, empathy, and restoration.

Each completed quest contributes to the restoration of the Living Resonance Network while rewarding exploration and curiosity.

---

# Quest 1 — The Forgotten Guardian

## Objective

Restore a Guardian Echo that has remained inactive for centuries.

### Tasks

- Locate the damaged Guardian Core.
- Collect three Resonance Fragments.
- Repair the Guardian using Echo Energy.
- Witness the Guardian's preserved memories.

### Rewards

- Guardian Sigil
- Lore Archive Entry
- Permanent Resonance increase

---

# Quest 2 — Lost Family Archive

## Objective

Recover scattered Memory Fragments belonging to an Elyrian family.

### Tasks

- Explore multiple chambers.
- Reconstruct fragmented memories.
- Restore the complete family archive.

### Rewards

- Hidden Reflection Memory
- Family Chronicle
- Achievement: "Remembered Together"

---

# Quest 3 — Echo Garden Restoration

## Objective

Revive the Crystal Garden surrounding the Hall.

### Tasks

- Restore Resonance Veins.
- Synchronize Crystal Seeds.
- Remove corrupted Echo growth.

### Rewards

- Environmental transformation.
- Rare Resonance Seed.
- New exploration path.

# Quest 4 — Song of Harmony

## Objective

### Rewards

# Quest 5 — The Silent Scholar

## Objective

Complete the unfinished research of an Elyrian scholar.

### Tasks

- Recover missing journals.
- Restore damaged Resonance diagrams.
- Reconstruct the scholar's final discovery.

### Rewards

- Scientific Archive
- Echo Ability Upgrade
- Additional Story Dialogue

---

# Quest Philosophy

Every side quest should:

- Expand world-building.
- Reveal personal stories.
- Reward exploration.
- Restore part of the Hall.
- Reinforce Project ECHO's themes.

No side quest should exist solely to increase playtime.

Each one should leave the player with a deeper appreciation of the Elyrian civilization.
