# History of the Silent Expanse

## Overview

The history of the Silent Expanse is inseparable from the history of Aethera itself. Long before the rise of the modern kingdoms, this region was the heartland of an advanced civilization whose achievements surpassed anything known today. Their cities reached into the skies, their machines harnessed Echo Energy, and their knowledge transformed the world.

Then, without warning, everything ended.

The civilization disappeared in a single event remembered only as **The Great Silence**.

To this day, no universally accepted explanation exists.

---

# Before the Great Silence

Thousands of years before the current era, the region now called the Silent Expanse was considered the center of civilization.

Its people mastered:

- Echo Energy
- Architecture
- Engineering
- Astronomy
- Medicine
- Long-distance communication
- Transportation

Many historians believe every modern kingdom inherited fragments of this civilization's knowledge.

---

# The Age of Prosperity

Historical records describe an era of peace unlike any other.

Cities flourished.

Trade connected distant lands.

Scientific discoveries accelerated every generation.

Massive structures, many of which still stand today, were constructed during this period.

Modern engineers cannot explain how these buildings were created.

---

# The Great Silence

Then everything changed.

No declaration of war.

No invasion.

No famine.

No plague.

No impact crater.

No volcanic eruption.

One day...

the civilization simply stopped.

Entire cities were abandoned.

Machines continued operating without their creators.

Meals remained on tables.

Doors stood open.

Tools lay where they had been dropped.

It appeared as though millions of people had vanished simultaneously.

---

# The Age of Fear

The civilizations that emerged afterward feared the ruins.

Stories spread of strange lights.

Voices with no speakers.

People disappearing without explanation.

Entire expeditions failing to return.

As generations passed, the region became known as **The Silent Expanse**.

---

# The First Expeditions

Centuries later, brave explorers attempted to uncover the truth.

Some recovered remarkable artifacts.

Others mapped ancient ruins.

Many disappeared forever.

Those who returned often described impossible events:

- Buildings changing shape.
- Hallways leading somewhere different each visit.
- Echo Storms rewriting the landscape.
- Memories that conflicted with one another.

These reports only deepened the mystery.

---

# Modern Era

Today, every kingdom studies the Silent Expanse, but none fully understand it.

Scholars search for knowledge.

Merchants seek forgotten treasures.

Governments fund expeditions.

Adventurers chase glory.

Yet the Expanse continues to guard its secrets.

Even after thousands of years, it has revealed only fragments of its true history.

---

# Historical Mysteries

Many questions remain unanswered.

- What caused the Great Silence?
- Where did the ancient civilization go?
- Why do some ruins remain perfectly preserved?
- Why does Echo Energy behave differently here?
- Who built the colossal structures beneath the Expanse?
- Are there still survivors hidden somewhere within?

These mysteries form the foundation of countless theories but remain unresolved.
