# Kingdom of Eryndor

## Overview

The Kingdom of Eryndor, known as the Emerald Kingdom, is one of the five sovereign kingdoms of Valenor. Renowned for its ancient forests, skilled rangers, and deep respect for nature, Eryndor has preserved its natural landscapes for centuries. Rather than expanding through conquest, the kingdom values harmony between civilization and the wilderness. It is considered the guardian of Valenor's forests and biodiversity.

---

## General Information

- **Official Name:** Kingdom of Eryndor
- **Capital:** Sylvaris
- **Government:** High Warden and Council of Elders
- **Current Ruler:** High Warden Elowen Thorne
- **Population:** Approximately 3.8 Million
- **Currency:** Valen Crown
- **National Symbol:** Silver Stag
- **Official Colors:** Emerald Green and Silver
- **Motto:** *"In Nature, We Endure."*

---

## Geography

Eryndor occupies the eastern forests of Valenor and contains some of the oldest surviving woodlands on Aethera. Massive trees, winding rivers, hidden waterfalls, mist-covered valleys, and sacred groves dominate the landscape. Most settlements are carefully integrated into the environment, minimizing their impact on nature.

---

## Climate

The kingdom experiences a cool temperate climate with abundant rainfall throughout the year. Dense forests create naturally shaded environments with frequent morning mist and moderate temperatures.

---

## Major Cities

### Sylvaris (Capital)

The political and cultural heart of Eryndor, built among towering ancient trees. Government buildings, ranger headquarters, and sacred groves coexist within the city.

### Green Hollow

A peaceful agricultural settlement specializing in medicinal herbs and forest crops.

### Elderwood

Known for its scholars, historians, and preservation of ancient knowledge.

### Moonbrook

A riverside town famous for fishing, trade, and seasonal festivals.

### Fernwatch

A frontier settlement where rangers monitor wildlife and protect the kingdom's borders.

---

## Government

Eryndor is governed by a High Warden supported by the Council of Elders. Rather than absolute rule, major political decisions are reached through consultation among experienced leaders representing different regions of the kingdom.

The government emphasizes conservation, stability, and responsible management of natural resources.

---

## Royal Leadership

### High Warden Elowen Thorne

Elowen Thorne serves as the protector of Eryndor rather than a traditional monarch. Known for wisdom, diplomacy, and exceptional leadership, the High Warden is responsible for safeguarding both the kingdom and its natural heritage.

---

## Military

The military consists primarily of the **Wardens of Eryndor**, elite rangers trained in:

- Long-range archery
- Forest navigation
- Guerrilla warfare
- Wildlife tracking
- Reconnaissance
- Survival skills

Unlike Ardentia's heavily armored knights, Eryndor's forces rely on speed, stealth, and intimate knowledge of the terrain.

---

## Economy

Eryndor maintains a sustainable economy built upon its natural resources.

Primary industries include:

- Sustainable forestry
- Herbal medicine
- Agriculture
- Hunting
- Archery craftsmanship
- Rare medicinal plants
- Eco-friendly trade

Strict laws prevent overexploitation of forests.

---

## Religion

Nature plays a central role in Eryndor's spiritual traditions. Sacred groves, ancient trees, and natural landmarks are treated with great respect.

Common places of worship include:

- Great World Tree
- Moon Groves
- Ancient Spirit Shrines

Although many legends speak of forest spirits, their existence remains part of the kingdom's cultural heritage rather than confirmed historical fact.

---

## Culture

Life in Eryndor emphasizes balance, patience, and respect for nature.

Citizens celebrate seasonal festivals, practice sustainable living, and pass down traditions through storytelling and music. Hunting is carefully regulated, and every harvested resource is expected to be used responsibly.

Hospitality toward travelers is valued, provided visitors respect the forests.

---

## Architecture

Buildings are constructed primarily from sustainably harvested timber and local stone.

Villages blend naturally into the landscape, while the capital, Sylvaris, is built around enormous ancient trees connected by wooden bridges, elevated walkways, and observation towers.

---

## Political Relations

### Ardentia

Friendly diplomatic relations exist, although disagreements occasionally arise regarding expansion into forested regions.

### Vespera

Strong trade partnership focused on timber, herbs, and crafted goods.

### Caelmoor

Mutual respect based on shared responsibility for protecting Valenor's borders.

### Drakmor

Relations remain cautious due to concerns over mining activities near environmentally sensitive areas.

---

## Story Importance

Eryndor serves as one of the major kingdoms explored during **ECHO: The Living World**. The kingdom introduces players to Valenor's natural ecosystems, ranger culture, ancient traditions, and forgotten mysteries hidden deep within the forests.

Several important quests and historical discoveries originate within Eryndor's wilderness.
