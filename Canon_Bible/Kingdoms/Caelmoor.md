# Kingdom of Caelmoor

## Overview

The Kingdom of Caelmoor is one of the five sovereign kingdoms of Valenor and serves as the continent's northern frontier. Positioned between the civilized kingdoms and the untamed wilderness, Caelmoor has spent centuries defending Valenor from external threats. Its people are known for their resilience, courage, and unwavering sense of duty. Life on the frontier is difficult, but the people of Caelmoor have built a proud kingdom where strength is measured not by wealth, but by perseverance.

---

## General Information

- **Official Name:** Kingdom of Caelmoor
- **Capital:** Thornwall
- **Government:** Constitutional Monarchy
- **Current Monarch:** Queen Rowan Ashford
- **Population:** Approximately 3.2 Million
- **Currency:** Valen Crown
- **National Symbol:** Iron Wolf
- **Official Colors:** Dark Green and Bronze
- **Motto:** *"Stand Until Dawn."*

---

## Geography

Caelmoor consists of rolling highlands, rocky hills, dense pine forests, cold rivers, and expansive grasslands. Much of its territory borders dangerous wilderness, making it one of the most strategically important kingdoms in Valenor.

---

## Climate

The kingdom experiences cold winters, cool summers, and frequent mountain winds. Harsh weather has shaped the resilience of its people for generations.

---

## Major Cities

### Thornwall (Capital)

The fortified capital of Caelmoor and the headquarters of the Frontier Guard.

### Wolfpine

A military settlement guarding the northern passes.

### Stonebrook

An agricultural town supplying food to the kingdom's frontier forts.

### Highmoor

Known for horse breeding and military training.

### Frostford

A riverside settlement that protects important trade crossings.

---

## Government

Caelmoor is governed by a constitutional monarchy supported by the Frontier Council, which includes military commanders, regional governors, and representatives from the frontier settlements.

The government prioritizes security, infrastructure, and the welfare of frontier communities.

---

## Royal Leadership

### Queen Rowan Ashford

Queen Rowan is respected as a practical and courageous leader who regularly visits remote settlements and military outposts. She believes that every citizen plays a role in protecting the kingdom.

---

## Military

The kingdom's military is known as the **Frontier Guard**.

Its forces specialize in:

- Border defense
- Cavalry patrols
- Wilderness survival
- Monster response
- Rapid reinforcement
- Defensive fortifications

The Frontier Guard is respected across Valenor for its discipline and ability to respond quickly to threats.

---

## Economy

Caelmoor maintains a stable economy supported by:

- Agriculture
- Livestock
- Horse breeding
- Timber
- Stone quarrying
- Leatherworking
- Frontier trade

Although not the wealthiest kingdom, it is largely self-sufficient.

---

## Religion

The people of Caelmoor value faith, family, and community. Small shrines and frontier chapels can be found throughout the kingdom, offering comfort to travelers and soldiers alike.

---

## Culture

The people of Caelmoor are hardworking, humble, and fiercely loyal. Festivals celebrate harvests, military victories, and the founding of frontier settlements.

Hospitality is considered a sacred duty, especially toward travelers crossing dangerous lands.

---

## Architecture

Caelmoor's architecture emphasizes practicality and defense. Stone keeps, wooden watchtowers, fortified villages, and sturdy bridges are common throughout the kingdom.

Buildings are designed to withstand harsh weather and potential attacks.

---

## Political Relations

### Ardentia

Strong alliance based on mutual defense and agricultural trade.

### Eryndor

Friendly relations through shared respect for protecting Valenor's natural borders.

### Drakmor

Military cooperation and engineering partnerships strengthen frontier fortifications.

### Vespera

Maintains supply routes and commercial exchanges supporting frontier settlements.

---

## Story Importance

Caelmoor introduces players to the dangers beyond civilization. Many quests involve defending villages, investigating mysterious attacks, escorting caravans, and uncovering secrets hidden in the northern frontier.
