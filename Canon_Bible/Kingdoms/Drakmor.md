# Kingdom of Drakmor

## Overview

The Kingdom of Drakmor is one of the five sovereign kingdoms of Valenor. Located within the towering Ironpeak Mountains, Drakmor is renowned for its master blacksmiths, rich mineral deposits, and nearly impregnable fortresses. Generations of miners, engineers, and warriors have transformed the harsh mountain landscape into one of the strongest industrial powers on the continent.

---

## General Information

- **Official Name:** Kingdom of Drakmor
- **Capital:** Ironreach
- **Government:** Constitutional Monarchy
- **Current Monarch:** King Darius Blackstone
- **Population:** Approximately 4.1 Million
- **Currency:** Valen Crown
- **National Symbol:** Iron Dragon
- **Official Colors:** Black and Crimson
- **Motto:** *"Strength Through Stone."*

---

## Geography

Drakmor occupies the western mountain ranges of Valenor. Massive peaks, deep valleys, underground cave systems, and mineral-rich cliffs dominate the kingdom. Most settlements are built directly into the mountains, making them naturally defensible.

---

## Climate

Drakmor experiences long, cold winters and short, cool summers. Snow covers the highest peaks for most of the year, while the valleys remain suitable for permanent settlements.

---

## Major Cities

### Ironreach (Capital)

The political, industrial, and military heart of Drakmor. The city is famous for its enormous fortress, royal foundries, and underground tunnels.

### Stonegate

A heavily fortified mountain city guarding the kingdom's western pass.

### Emberforge

The center of metallurgy and advanced weapon production.

### Frosthold

A northern settlement known for mining rare minerals.

### Deepvault

A vast underground city built within ancient caverns.

---

## Government

Drakmor is governed by a constitutional monarchy supported by a Royal Council composed of military commanders, guild leaders, engineers, and regional governors.

The kingdom values efficiency, discipline, and long-term planning.

---

## Royal Leadership

### King Darius Blackstone

A respected ruler known for military discipline, engineering innovation, and economic development. His leadership focuses on strengthening Drakmor without seeking unnecessary wars.

---

## Military

The **Iron Legion** forms the backbone of Drakmor's armed forces.

Specializations include:

- Heavy infantry
- Shield formations
- Siege engineering
- Mountain warfare
- Defensive fortifications
- Elite royal guards

Unlike Eryndor's mobile rangers, Drakmor's soldiers excel in endurance, defense, and organized battlefield tactics.

---

## Economy

Drakmor possesses one of Valenor's strongest industrial economies.

Primary industries include:

- Mining
- Blacksmithing
- Steel production
- Engineering
- Stone masonry
- Precious gemstones
- Heavy manufacturing

Its weapons, armor, and tools are exported throughout Valenor.

---

## Religion

The people of Drakmor honor perseverance, craftsmanship, and ancestral legacy.

Temples are often carved directly into mountains and serve as both places of worship and historical archives.

---

## Culture

Drakmor's society values discipline, honesty, hard work, and family tradition.

Children often learn mining, engineering, or smithing from an early age. Festivals celebrate craftsmanship, military victories, and the completion of major engineering projects.

---

## Architecture

Massive stone fortresses, underground halls, fortified bridges, towering walls, and carved mountain cities define Drakmor's architecture.

Buildings are designed for durability over decoration.

---

## Political Relations

### Ardentia

Strong diplomatic and trade relations based on military cooperation and commerce.

### Eryndor

Relations remain cautious due to disagreements over mining activities near protected forests.

### Vespera

Important trading partner for exporting metals, weapons, and industrial goods.

### Caelmoor

Cooperates on border defense and military logistics.

---

## Story Importance

Drakmor introduces players to Valenor's industrial power, engineering achievements, and military traditions. Many important quests involve ancient underground ruins, forgotten mines, political intrigue, and powerful relics hidden beneath the mountains.
