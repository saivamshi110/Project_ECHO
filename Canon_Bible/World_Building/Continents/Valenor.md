# Valenor

## Overview

Valenor, known as the Crown of Aethera, is the primary continent of ECHO: The Living World and the setting of the first game. It is the political, cultural, and economic heart of Aethera, home to five major kingdoms, an independent city-state, and vast unexplored wilderness. Following the Great Silence, Valenor became one of the first regions to rebuild civilization and remains the most influential continent in the known world.

---

- **Continent:** Valenor

---

## Geography

Valenor features a wide variety of landscapes, including fertile plains, dense forests, towering mountain ranges, rugged coastlines, wetlands, and ancient ruins. Its geography supports diverse cultures, wildlife, and ecosystems.

---

## Climate

The continent experiences multiple climate zones:

- Northern snowy mountains
- Western coastal regions
- Central fertile plains
- Eastern ancient forests
- Southern wetlands
- Southeastern Echo-blighted lands

---

## Political Structure

Valenor consists of five sovereign kingdoms that govern their own territories while maintaining diplomatic relations through trade, treaties, and military alliances.

The kingdoms are:

- Ardentia
- Eryndor
- Drakmor
- Vespera
- Caelmoor

---

## Independent Regions

Although most of Valenor is ruled by kingdoms, some regions remain politically independent.

- Asterfall — Neutral city-state
- The Silent Expanse — Unclaimed and highly dangerous

## Natural Resources

Valenor possesses fertile farmland, timber, minerals, fresh water, fisheries, and rare Echo Crystals that have shaped its economy and history.

---

## Wildlife

The continent is home to ordinary animals as well as Echo-altered creatures found in remote regions. Different ecosystems support unique wildlife adapted to forests, mountains, wetlands, and coastal areas.

---

## Economy

Trade between kingdoms drives Valenor's economy. Agriculture, mining, forestry, fishing, craftsmanship, and maritime commerce all contribute to regional prosperity.

---

## Culture

Each kingdom has developed its own traditions, architecture, festivals, clothing, and customs while sharing a common historical heritage dating back to the rebuilding after the Great Silence.

---

## Religion

Several faiths exist throughout Valenor, with different kingdoms favoring different traditions. Religious institutions influence politics, education, and everyday life.

---

## History

Valenor played a central role in rebuilding civilization after the Great Silence. Over centuries, kingdoms rose, alliances formed, wars reshaped borders, and new generations inherited the legacy of the past.

---

## Current Era

At the beginning of Game 1, Valenor enjoys an uneasy peace. Political tensions, ancient mysteries, and the lingering effects of Echo Energy threaten to disrupt the balance of the continent.

---

## Important Locations

Important locations include:

- Five Kingdoms
- Asterfall
- The Silent Expanse
- Ancient ruins

---

## Story Importance

Valenor serves as the primary setting for ECHO: The Living World (Game 1). The player's journey explores its kingdoms, cultures, conflicts, and mysteries while uncovering the deeper history of Aethera.
