\chapter{Target Audience}

\section{Overview}

ECHO: The Living World is designed for players who value immersive storytelling, meaningful exploration, intelligent AI interactions, and emotionally engaging gameplay. Rather than focusing solely on fast-paced action or competitive mechanics, ECHO aims to deliver a world that feels alive and responds naturally to player choices.

The game is intended to appeal to players who enjoy discovering hidden stories, building relationships with characters, and shaping the world through their decisions.

\section{Primary Audience}

The primary audience consists of players between the ages of 16 and 40 who enjoy narrative-driven open-world role-playing games.

These players typically appreciate:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Rich storytelling
    \item Exploration and world discovery
    \item Character-driven experiences
    \item Decision-based gameplay
    \item Long-form progression
    \item High replay value
\end{itemize}

\section{Player Types}

ECHO is designed to satisfy multiple styles of players.

\subsection{Explorers}

Players who enjoy uncovering hidden locations, ancient ruins, environmental storytelling, and world secrets.

\subsection{Story Lovers}

Players who seek emotional narratives, memorable characters, and meaningful relationships.

\subsection{Role Players}

Players who enjoy making choices that influence both their character and the world around them.

\subsection{Completionists}

Players motivated to discover every secret, complete every quest, and fully understand the history of Aethera.

\section{Target Platforms}

The initial release is planned for PC, with future consideration for PlayStation and Xbox platforms depending on project scope and available resources.

\section{Player Experience Goals}

The intended player experience can be summarized as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Feel emotionally connected to the world.
    \item Discover mysteries through exploration.
    \item Build lasting relationships with intelligent characters.
    \item Experience meaningful consequences for decisions.
    \item Leave the game with memorable stories rather than simply completed objectives.
\end{itemize}

\section{Player Emotional Journey}

Throughout the game, the player should experience a carefully designed emotional progression:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Curiosity -- "What is this world?"
    \item Wonder -- "This world feels alive."
    \item Attachment -- "I care about these people."
    \item Doubt -- "Who is actually right?"
    \item Loss -- "My choices have consequences."
    \item Hope -- "The future depends on what I decide."
\end{enumerate}

The objective is to create an experience that players remember not only for its gameplay, but also for the emotions it evokes long after completion.
