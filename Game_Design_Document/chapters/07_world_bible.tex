\chapter{World Bible}

\section{Overview of Aethera}

Aethera is the fictional planet on which \textit{ECHO: The Living World} takes place. It is an ancient world shaped by forgotten civilizations, evolving kingdoms, and the mysterious force known as Echo Energy.

Over thousands of years, nature has reclaimed the ruins of the past, leaving behind ancient cities, hidden structures, and relics whose true purpose has been lost to history. Every region of Aethera carries traces of those who came before, making exploration a journey through both the present and the past.

The world is designed to reward curiosity, discovery, and environmental storytelling. Every location, from a small village to an abandoned ruin, exists for a reason and contributes to the overall narrative of the world.

\section{World Structure}

Aethera follows a hybrid world design.

The planet consists of multiple continents, each with its own geography, cultures, political systems, wildlife, and history. However, the events of \textit{ECHO: The Living World} are focused entirely on a single continent, allowing the playable world to be rich in detail while preserving opportunities for future stories and expansions.

This approach creates the feeling of a living planet where unexplored lands continue to exist beyond the player's journey.

\section{The Continents of Aethera}

The planet is divided into six major continents.

\begin{itemize}
    \item Northern Continent
    \item Eastern Empire
    \item Southern Wildlands
    \item Western Frontier
    \item Frozen Isles
    \item Shattered Expanse
\end{itemize}

Each continent possesses unique civilizations, ecosystems, traditions, and historical events that have shaped the modern world.

Although these continents are part of Aethera's history and geography, only the Northern Continent serves as the primary setting for the first game.

\section{The Northern Continent}

The Northern Continent is the main playable region of \textit{ECHO: The Living World}.

It contains numerous kingdoms, thriving cities, peaceful villages, dense forests, mountain ranges, deserts, rivers, ancient ruins, and mysterious locations influenced by Echo Energy.

Most of the world's major political conflicts, historical discoveries, and story events occur within this continent. It serves as the center of exploration, adventure, and the player's journey throughout the game.

\section{History of the World}

Thousands of years before the events of the game, Aethera was home to an advanced civilization whose knowledge far exceeded that of the present age.

At the height of its prosperity, this civilization suddenly disappeared during a mysterious event remembered only as the Great Silence.

The true cause of the Great Silence has been forgotten over time. What remains are abandoned cities, hidden technologies, ancient relics, and scattered records waiting to be rediscovered.

The disappearance of this civilization became one of the greatest mysteries in Aethera's history and continues to influence the world long after its fall.

\section{Echo Energy}

Echo Energy is the fundamental force that exists throughout Aethera.

Unlike traditional forms of magic, Echo Energy does not create something from nothing. Instead, it preserves, reflects, amplifies, and transforms existing memories, emotions, actions, and energy.

The origins of Echo Energy remain unknown.

Different kingdoms interpret it in different ways. Some consider it sacred, others study it scientifically, while some fear its unpredictable nature.

The secrets surrounding Echo Energy form one of the central mysteries of the game.

\section{World Design Philosophy}

The world of Aethera is designed around meaningful exploration rather than simple map size.

Every settlement, landmark, ruin, cave, and environment is created with its own history, purpose, and narrative significance.

Players are encouraged to discover hidden stories through exploration, conversations, environmental details, and ancient relics instead of relying solely on direct storytelling.

The objective is to create a world that feels believable, interconnected, and alive.

\section{Summary}

Aethera is intended to feel like a living planet with centuries of history beyond the player's own adventure.

While the first game explores only the Northern Continent, the existence of additional continents provides a strong foundation for future stories, sequels, and expansions without limiting the scope of the world itself.

Every choice, every discovery, and every forgotten ruin contributes to the central philosophy of the game:

\begin{quote}
``Every choice leaves an Echo.''
\end{quote}
