\section{Overview}

This appendix contains supporting information, terminology, references, and supplementary material related to the development of \textit{ECHO: The Living World}.

\section{Glossary}

\begin{description}
    \item[Aethera] The fictional planet where the game takes place.
    \item[Echo Energy] The mysterious force influencing the world's history and gameplay.
    \item[Great Silence] The unexplained disappearance of the ancient civilization.
    \item[NPC] Non-Playable Character.
    \item[AI] Artificial Intelligence.
    \item[RPG] Role-Playing Game.
    \item[GDD] Game Design Document.
\end{description}

\section{Game Inspiration}

The design of \textit{ECHO: The Living World} draws inspiration from successful open-world and role-playing games while establishing its own unique identity.

Inspirational design areas include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Open-world exploration
    \item Environmental storytelling
    \item Dynamic AI systems
    \item Meaningful player choices
    \item Cinematic narrative
\end{itemize}

\section{Closing Statement}

\textit{ECHO: The Living World} represents a long-term vision of creating a deeply immersive open-world role-playing experience where exploration, intelligent systems, meaningful choices, and emotional storytelling combine to form a believable living world.

Every journey leaves a memory.

Every action leaves a consequence.
