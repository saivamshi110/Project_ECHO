\chapter{AI Bible}

\section{Overview}

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is one of the core pillars of \textit{ECHO: The Living World}. Rather than acting as simple scripted entities, AI-controlled characters are designed to behave naturally, react intelligently to the player's actions, and contribute to the feeling of a believable living world.

The AI system governs the behaviour of civilians, companions, enemies, wildlife, merchants, guards, and important story characters. Every AI entity follows behaviour rules appropriate to its role within the world.

The objective of the AI system is to create immersion by making the world feel active even when the player is not directly interacting with it.

\section{AI Design Philosophy}

The AI system is designed around the following principles:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Believable behaviour
    \item Context-aware decision making
    \item Dynamic reactions
    \item Realistic daily routines
    \item Adaptive combat behaviour
    \item Meaningful companion interactions
\end{itemize}

Rather than giving every character identical behaviour, each AI type has unique goals, responsibilities, and reactions.

\section{Civilian AI}

Civilian AI represents the ordinary people living throughout Aethera.

Citizens follow daily schedules such as working, shopping, resting, travelling, eating, and sleeping. They respond naturally to environmental events and player actions.

Depending on the player's reputation, civilians may become friendly, fearful, suspicious, or hostile.

\section{Companion AI}

Companions are designed to support the player throughout the adventure.

They assist during combat, explore alongside the player, participate in conversations, provide contextual observations, and react emotionally to important story events.

Companions remember significant player decisions, allowing their relationships with the player to evolve naturally over time.

\section{Enemy AI}

Enemy AI adapts to changing battlefield conditions instead of relying on repetitive attack patterns.

Enemies can:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Search for the player
    \item Take cover
    \item Coordinate attacks
    \item Protect allies
    \item Retreat when overwhelmed
    \item Defend important objectives
\end{itemize}

Different enemy factions possess unique combat strategies based on their training and culture.

\section{Wildlife AI}

Wildlife behaves according to natural instincts.

Animals search for food, avoid danger, defend their territory, migrate between habitats, and interact with other wildlife.

Predators hunt prey while herbivores generally avoid conflict unless threatened.

Wildlife behaviour contributes to making the environment feel alive and believable.

\section{Guard AI}

Guards maintain public order within settlements.

Their responsibilities include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Patrolling assigned routes
    \item Investigating suspicious activity
    \item Protecting important locations
    \item Pursuing criminals
    \item Assisting civilians during emergencies
\end{itemize}

Guard behaviour varies depending on the laws and culture of each kingdom.

\section{Boss AI}

Boss encounters are designed to provide unique gameplay experiences.

Bosses adapt to the player's combat style by changing attack patterns, movement strategies, and defensive behaviour throughout different phases of battle.

Each major boss possesses distinct abilities and combat mechanics that reflect their role within the story.

\section{Dialogue AI}

Dialogue interactions are influenced by story progression, player reputation, completed quests, and previous decisions.

Important characters may provide different responses depending on the player's actions, creating more personalized conversations throughout the game.

\section{Future AI Features}

Future versions of the AI system may include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Advanced memory systems
    \item Improved group coordination
    \item Dynamic world events
    \item Emotion-driven behaviour
    \item Adaptive learning systems
\end{itemize}

These features are considered long-term development goals for future iterations of the project.

\section{Summary}

The AI system of \textit{ECHO: The Living World} is designed to support the game's central vision of creating a believable, interactive, and emotionally engaging world.

Every AI entity, from ordinary villagers to powerful bosses, contributes to the living ecosystem of Aethera through intelligent behaviour, contextual decision making, and meaningful interactions with the player.
