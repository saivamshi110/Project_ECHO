\chapter{Game Overview}

\section{High Concept}

ECHO: The Living World is a next-generation story-driven open-world RPG that combines emotional storytelling, dynamic artificial intelligence, and a persistent living world. Rather than following a fixed adventure, players experience a world where every decision, relationship, and action can influence future events.

The game is designed around the belief that stories become memorable when players feel emotionally connected to the people and places around them. Every journey should feel personal, with the player's choices shaping not only the narrative but also the lives of the characters they encounter.

\section{Game Identity}

ECHO is built upon five defining principles:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Story-driven gameplay with meaningful player choices.
    \item AI-powered characters that react, remember, and evolve.
    \item A persistent world that continues to change over time.
    \item Emotional relationships between the player and the world.
    \item Exploration that rewards curiosity instead of checklist completion.
\end{itemize}

\section{Player Experience}

Players should feel like they are living inside a believable world rather than completing a sequence of missions. Every village, forest, city, and character should contribute to an experience where exploration, discovery, and relationships are equally important.

Instead of asking "How do I finish this game?", the player should naturally begin asking, "What will happen next?"
