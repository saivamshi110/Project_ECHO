# Narrative Bible

> *"Every memory tells a story. Every story shapes a civilization."*

---

# Overview

The Narrative Bible defines the complete narrative framework of Project ECHO.

Unlike the Canon Bible, which documents the world itself, the Narrative Bible focuses on how the player experiences that world through story, characters, dialogue, pacing, and emotional progression.

It establishes the narrative rules that guide every chapter, location, quest, cinematic, and conversation throughout the game.

---

# Narrative Philosophy

Project ECHO tells a story about remembrance rather than revenge.

Its central themes include:

- Memory
- Legacy
- Identity
- Hope
- Responsibility
- Preservation

Players gradually uncover the truth of the Elyrian civilization, not through exposition alone, but by exploring memories, restoring forgotten places, and earning the trust of those who came before.

---

# Narrative Structure

The story is divided into multiple chapters that follow the player's emotional journey from curiosity to responsibility.

Each chapter introduces new Legendary Locations, gameplay systems, characters, and philosophical questions while expanding the player's understanding of the Living Resonance Network.

# Relationship to the Canon Bible

The Canon Bible defines what exists within Project ECHO.

The Narrative Bible defines how players discover, understand, and emotionally connect with those elements.

Together they form the complete narrative foundation for the game.
