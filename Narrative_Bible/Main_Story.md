# Main Story

## Premise

Long after the fall of the Elyrian civilization, the Living Resonance Network continues to preserve memories scattered across the Silent Expanse.

The player awakens in a world where countless Legendary Locations remain dormant, their knowledge locked behind fractured Echo pathways and forgotten Resonance systems.

Guided by fragments of preserved memories, the player gradually restores these ancient places, uncovering the truth behind the Elyrians, the creation of Echo Energy, and the purpose of the Living Resonance Network.

As more memories return, it becomes clear that the player is not merely exploring history.

They are becoming its next guardian.

---

## Central Conflict

The greatest danger facing the world is not physical destruction.

It is the loss of memory.

As the Living Resonance Network continues to weaken, entire civilizations risk disappearing forever.

The player's journey is therefore one of restoration rather than conquest.

---

## Narrative Goal

The player must restore the Living Resonance Network, awaken the Legendary Locations, earn the title of Keeper of Echoes, and ensure that the knowledge of the Elyrian civilization survives for future generations.
