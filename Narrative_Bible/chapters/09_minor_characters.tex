\chapter{Minor Characters}

\section{Overview}

Minor characters provide life to the world through meaningful conversations, local stories, professions, and personal struggles.

\section{Citizens}

Farmers, merchants, craftsmen, guards, sailors, and scholars each contribute to the atmosphere of their settlements.

\section{Quest NPCs}

Many quests originate from ordinary individuals rather than powerful rulers.

These stories reveal the everyday consequences of political conflict and historical events.

\section{Recurring Characters}

Certain minor characters may appear throughout multiple chapters of the game, allowing players to witness how their lives change over time.

\section{Living World Philosophy}

NPC schedules, conversations, occupations, and relationships should evolve naturally in response to world events whenever possible.
