\chapter{Languages and Cultures}

\section{Overview}

The people of Aethera possess diverse customs shaped by geography, history, trade, and political traditions.

Although many regional dialects exist, several major languages dominate international communication.

\section{Common Tongue}

The Common Tongue serves as the primary language for diplomacy, commerce, education, and travel across Valenor.

\section{Ancient Echo Script}

The Ancient Echo Script is an extinct written language found within ruins dating back to the Age of Harmony.

Modern scholars have only partially deciphered its symbols.

\section{Regional Traditions}

Each kingdom maintains unique traditions, architecture, clothing styles, festivals, and methods of governance.

These cultural differences influence dialogue, social customs, and quest design throughout the game.

\section{Hospitality and Honor}

Honor, reputation, and hospitality remain important social values throughout much of Valenor.

A person's actions often determine how communities respond to them, reinforcing the world's reactive narrative philosophy.

\section{Cultural Identity}

Every civilization should possess a distinct identity that can be recognized through architecture, music, clothing, language, cuisine, and storytelling.
