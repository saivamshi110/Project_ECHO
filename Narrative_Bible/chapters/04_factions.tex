\chapter{Factions}

\section{Overview}

Numerous factions compete for influence across Aethera. Their goals often conflict, creating political tension, alliances, and rivalries that shape the world's future.

\section{Kingdoms}

Independent kingdoms govern large regions of each continent. Their relationships range from peaceful cooperation to open conflict.

\section{Merchant Guilds}

Trade organizations control commerce between continents, transporting goods, knowledge, and rare Echo artifacts.

\section{Explorers' Guild}

Dedicated to discovering ancient ruins and recovering forgotten knowledge from before the Great Silence.

\section{Scholars of Echo}

An organization devoted to researching Echo Energy. Members seek to understand its origins while preventing its misuse.

\section{Independent Mercenaries}

Professional warriors and adventurers available for hire by kingdoms, merchants, or private individuals.

\section{Summary}

Factions create a dynamic political landscape where cooperation and conflict continually reshape the world.
