\chapter{Major Characters}

\section{Overview}

The central narrative follows individuals whose decisions shape the future of Aethera.

Unlike traditional fantasy heroes, no character is inherently destined to save the world.

Their choices, relationships, and personal beliefs determine their legacy.

\section{The Protagonist}

The player controls an unnamed traveler whose past is intentionally left open, allowing players to define aspects of their own identity throughout the journey.

The protagonist is neither royalty nor a chosen hero.

Instead, they become significant through the decisions they make.

\section{Primary Supporting Cast}

The supporting cast consists of companions, rulers, scholars, merchants, and explorers whose personal motivations often conflict with one another.

Relationships evolve naturally according to player decisions.

\section{Antagonistic Forces}

Opposition originates from political conflicts, ideological disagreements, ancient secrets, and individuals seeking to control Echo Energy for their own purposes.

There is no singular embodiment of evil.

Instead, every major antagonist believes their actions are justified.
