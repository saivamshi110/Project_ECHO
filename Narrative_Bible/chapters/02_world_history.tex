\chapter{World History}

\section{The World of Aethera}

Aethera is an ancient world shaped by countless civilizations over thousands of years. Long before the current kingdoms emerged, powerful societies mastered the mysterious force known as Echo Energy.

Echo Energy transformed technology, architecture, communication, and warfare, allowing civilizations to flourish beyond ordinary limitations.

\section{The Age of Harmony}

During this era, nations cooperated to explore and refine Echo Energy. Knowledge spread across continents, cities prospered, and great monuments were constructed.

Many ruins found throughout Aethera originate from this period.

\section{The Great Silence}

The greatest catastrophe in recorded history became known as the Great Silence.

Without warning, the civilizations that controlled Echo Energy collapsed. Entire cities disappeared, communication ceased, and vast regions were abandoned.

The true cause remains unknown.

Only scattered ruins and fragments of knowledge survived.

\section{The Age of Recovery}

Centuries later, new kingdoms rose upon the remnants of the ancient world.

Modern civilizations rediscovered fragments of Echo technology but lacked the knowledge to fully understand its origins.

Political conflicts, exploration, and competing ambitions now define this new era.

\section{Current Era}

The events of \textit{ECHO: The Living World} begin during this period of uncertainty, where the past remains buried beneath forgotten ruins and every discovery has the potential to reshape the future.
