\chapter{Religions and Beliefs}

\section{Overview}

Religion in Aethera is diverse, shaped by centuries of history, culture, and the mysterious influence of Echo Energy. While different civilizations worship different deities and philosophies, most share one common belief: every action leaves a lasting Echo upon the world.

\section{The Echo Doctrine}

The most widespread belief across Valenor is the Echo Doctrine.

Followers believe that every thought, action, and decision creates an invisible Echo that influences both the individual and the world around them. Acts of kindness strengthen harmony, while cruelty and greed leave scars that may endure for generations.

The doctrine teaches personal responsibility rather than divine punishment.

\section{The Church of Lumina}

The Church of Lumina worships Solis, the eternal sun, as the symbol of hope, justice, and renewal.

Its priests maintain temples throughout Valenor and often serve as healers, historians, and advisors to noble families.

\section{The Moon Keepers}

The Moon Keepers revere Lunara, believing the moon preserves memories that have been lost through time.

Many scholars seek guidance from the Moon Keepers when studying ancient Echo ruins.

\section{The Forgotten Ones}

Scattered cults worship forgotten beings believed to have existed before the Great Silence.

Most kingdoms outlaw these organizations due to their dangerous experiments with corrupted Echo Energy.

\section{Religious Diversity}

No single religion dominates the world. Faith varies between kingdoms, cultures, and individuals, contributing to the diversity of Aethera.
