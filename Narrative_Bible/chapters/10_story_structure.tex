\chapter{Story Structure}

\section{Narrative Philosophy}

The story emphasizes player agency, exploration, discovery, and consequence.

Rather than presenting a strictly linear narrative, multiple paths allow players to influence political alliances, personal relationships, and regional outcomes.

\section{Act Structure}

The main story is divided into five narrative acts.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Arrival in Valenor
    \item Discovery of Echo Ruins
    \item Political Conflict
    \item Secrets of the Great Silence
    \item The Final Echo
\end{enumerate}

\section{Side Stories}

Optional quests expand the world's history, cultures, and individual characters without interrupting the main narrative.

Many side stories influence future dialogue and regional relationships.

\section{Multiple Outcomes}

Player choices determine alliances, faction influence, character survival, and portions of the ending.

No single ending is considered the universally correct outcome.

\section{Narrative Consistency}

Every quest should reinforce the central philosophy of the game:

\begin{quote}
Every choice leaves an Echo.
\end{quote}
