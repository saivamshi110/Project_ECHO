\chapter{Regions}

\section{Planet}

The world is known as Aethera.

Its landscapes range from frozen islands to tropical jungles, ancient forests, towering mountains, deserts, and forgotten ruins.

\section{Major Continents}

The six primary continents are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Valenor
    \item Kharath
    \item Solvara
    \item Myrhaven
    \item Nivalis
    \item Ashkar
\end{itemize}

\section{Valenor}

Valenor serves as the primary setting for the first installment of the series.

It contains ancient kingdoms, forests, mountains, abandoned ruins, and remnants of civilizations lost during the Great Silence.

\section{The Great Oceans}

The continents are separated by vast oceans including:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Azure Expanse
    \item Frost Sea
    \item Ember Gulf
    \item Veil Ocean
\end{itemize}

\section{Exploration Philosophy}

Every region should possess a unique identity, history, culture, architecture, and environmental storytelling that encourages exploration and discovery.
